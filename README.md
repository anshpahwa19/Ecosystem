## Direction B, in brief

- **Symbol.** Five copies of the wordmark's first o (traced from `src/assets/bloom-wordmark-alpha.png`, outline smoothed to within 0.5% of the glyph), each touching one centre and woven over its neighbour. The petal paths are the `#b-p0`–`#b-p4` paths in the SVG sprite. The wordmark itself is unchanged.
- **Colour.** Eight base colours, each sampled from something Bloom already owns: Oxblood `#932A23` (wordmark), Ink `#1C1A19` (Bloom Partners headings), Paper `#F9F9F9` (Bloom Partners page), White, Sandstone `#85674B`, Garden `#4B5715` and Dusk `#684FA1` (deepened from the evening sky `#A79DBF`) and Marina `#47699E` (deepened from the marina sky `#7C8DA9`), all from Bloom Universal photography. Sample points are recorded in `WINS` in the source.
- **Type.** Cormorant Garamond for headlines, Plus Jakarta Sans for interface text, lining figures throughout.
- **Symbol rules.** The symbol only signs: whole, in one colour (oxblood, or white on a dark field), in lockups, app icons and favicons. It is never recoloured in parts, never a chart, tracker or loader, and never a bullet in text.
- **Product views.** One Bloom, six views: no new marks. Each product is a crop of the same flower (`VIEWS` in the source): Universal the whole flower, Homes one petal's arch, Community the centre where all five meet, Partners the wordmark's two o's woven together, Multiverse the wordmark's m, traced as it is. App icons show the view white on oxblood; lockup rows end with the same view, oxblood on cream.
- **Product screens.** The five web concept screens (07e, 07f and the full-size view), the three app screens (07g–07i) and 07d's product cards share one product language: frosted glass panels, bars and cards over warm paper lit by soft glows in each product's accent and in oxblood; oxblood pills for primary actions and selected states; Cormorant display headlines over Plus Jakarta Sans controls. The web navigation is a floating glass pill whose white bubble springs to the link under the pointer; the apps have a floating glass app bar and a floating glass tab dock. Motion runs on four tempos (micro .18s, component .34s, reveal .56s, signature .95s): reveals rise out of a soft blur, staggered from each screen's focal point, and replay whenever a screen comes into view; the glows drift slowly where a screen is shown on its own. Reduced motion stops all of it. In the full-size view the web screens are live: choose a business on Universal, filter facades on the master plan, pick a request type on Partners, approve or reject tasks on Multiverse; 07d's approval card works on the slide.
- **App concepts that scroll.** The Partners, Home-buying and Community concept screens are whole home screens in one feed that scrolls under the floating glass app bar and tab dock (scroll inside the phone): Partners carries the featured project, leads performance, commission, team members, marketing material and team performance with each agent; Homes the appointment, Al Metlaa, and the available units with prices. One 12px rhythm, 20-24px corners, serif figures, as on the web screens. Content is from the supplied screens.
- **Updated web screens, as whole pages.** Direction B now draws the updated Bloom Partners dashboard (John Doe), the home-buying home page (Javed, Al Metlaa) and Bloom Education (`src/screens/bloom-partners-dashboard.webp`, `home-buying-home.webp`, `bloom-education.webp`; source 18) as long pages in the Multiverse grammar: a hero the slides show, the rest scrolling in the full-size view. 07e compares them with those screens and gains an Education tab; 07f shows six products (`PRODUCTS_B`). Direction A and the 05 analysis slides keep the earlier screens.
- **Bloom Universal, an expanding gallery.** The six businesses stand side by side as photo panels: HD photographs supplied for the concept (illustrative, not Bloom's own sites; `src/screens/universal-*.webp`, cropped to 720 x 1006) for all six businesses; the chosen one opens wide with its name and an Explore pill while the rest stand as labelled slivers. Hover (real pointer movement only), the arrows beside Let's get started (with a rolling 01 / 06 counter) or the keyboard choose one; in the full-size view a progress line on the open panel moves to the next business every six seconds and pauses while the pointer is on the gallery. The line is a CSS animation, so reduced motion leaves the gallery still, and the 07f thumbnail never moves.
- **Bloom Multiverse, the whole page.** Direction B's Multiverse concept is the employee home as one long page, in the order the live page runs: a light hero (the greeting, two actions, the four apps on a glass orbit around the 32 actions waiting), then attention required, new joiners, announcements, policies, Life at Bloom, discounts, perks, questions and the footer (`mvPage` in the source). The slides show only its first 874 x 454, the hero. Selecting it on 07f opens the whole page in the full-size view: it opens on the hero and scrolls; the glass navigation rides the scroll on the compositor (a scroll-driven animation, with a frame-synced fallback) and, once past the hero, squeezes into a compact, denser glass pill; each section rises in as one piece as it arrives (glass below the hero drops its blur, which shows nothing over flat paper, so scrolling stays smooth), and the page is live (filter tasks by app and approve or reject them, filter policies, pick a perk, open an answer, jump to the tasks and back to the top). Content is only what today's page shows; where its text cannot be read, the concept uses section labels instead of inventing it, and brands other than the two Bloom hotels are text labels.

## Mobile apps

- **Screens.** Home screens supplied by the Bloom team: Bloom Partners (`src/screens/bloom-partners-app.webp`, 818 px wide), the home-buying app (`home-buying-app.jpg`, embedded as supplied) and the Bloom Community App (`bloom-community-app.webp`, straightened out of a promotional device frame, its bezel corners painted over). In the phone frames the content scrolls while each app's fixed parts stay pinned: status bars, Partners' tab bar and the floating Book Appointment.
- **05e–05g, today.** The three apps in phone frames; the same jobs side by side (Bloom in view, greeting, main action, navigation, colour); and what it means: 1 app with the wordmark in view, 3 greeting styles, 3 navigation patterns, 5 reds and none on a main action.
- **07g–07i, direction B.** 07g puts today's screen beside the concept on a dark stage lit in the app's accent, then points out each change on the phone. 07h is the app shell's anatomy on oxblood: a floating glass app bar signed by the symbol and wordmark, serif greeting, one accent, glass cards, one oxblood pill floating above a glass tab dock, each with a leader line into the phone. It is tuned per app: Partners in Dusk, Homes in Sandstone, Community in Garden. 07i shows the three concepts together on one stage with their icons. Concept screens are drawn at 390 × 844 (`MOBILE` in the source) with content from today's screens and the Bloom Community App page.
